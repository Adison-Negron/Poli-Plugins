# Poli-Multiband factory presets

Pick them from the **PRESET** menu at the top of the plugin's screen on the MPC. Generated by `presets/make_presets.py` from `presets/factory.json`.

The presets were tuned on reference audio (presets/README.md): vocals peaking at -3 dBFS, a drum loop at -1 dBFS, finished mixes at their own level. If your track is much quieter or louder, move each band's **Thresh** so the green meter goes past the white bar about as much as described below. *Level* = how much louder or quieter than the dry signal the preset played on its reference.
